Advertising Policies & Standards

  • Strictly prohibited to publish ads containing illegal, pornographic, gambling, or other harmful content
  • Ad content must be truthful and credible, without false or misleading information
  • Ad creatives must comply with relevant laws, regulations and industry standards
  • All ad content will be reviewed before going live

Partnership Commitment

  • Guarantee the agreed ad placement and display frequency
  • Provide detailed ad performance data reports regularly
  • Offer professional optimization suggestions based on campaign performance
  • Respect user experience, ensuring ads don't interfere with website browsing

User Privacy Protection

  • Strictly adhere to privacy protection regulations, safeguarding user data security

Frequently Asked Questions

Our standard advertising placement has a minimum period of one quarter (three months), which ensures the ad has sufficient display time to achieve the desired effect. For special requirements, you can choose a custom solution for negotiation.

We provide detailed data analysis reports, including impressions, click-through rate, conversion rate, and other core metrics. Premium and custom solutions also provide user behavior analysis and audience profiles to help you more comprehensively evaluate ad effectiveness.

Different plans have different update frequency regulations. The Basic Display plan allows updates once a month, the Premium Display plan allows updates twice a month. If you choose a custom solution, you can update ad content as needed, specific details can be discussed with your account manager.

We support various ad formats, including image ads, text link ads, hybrid ads, etc. Custom solutions also offer advanced formats such as native ads and content collaborations. All ad formats will be adapted to the website style to ensure a good user experience.
